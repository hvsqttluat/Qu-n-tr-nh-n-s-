# Minh chung Figma UI/UX

Thu muc nay dung de chot bonus "co su dung Figma de thiet ke UI/UX".

## Cach thuc hien tren Figma

1. Mo Figma bang tai khoan cua nhom.
2. Tao file moi ten `QuanLyNhanSuWpf - UI UX`.
3. Keo file `Figma_UIUX_Wireframes.svg` vao canvas Figma.
4. Can chinh lai cac frame neu can va dat ten frame:
   - `01 Dang nhap`
   - `02 Tong quan`
   - `03 Quan ly nhan vien`
5. Bam `Share`, copy link Figma.
6. Dan link vao muc "Minh chung Figma" trong bao cao bonus/checklist.

## Noi dung thiet ke

Bo wireframe gom 3 man hinh dai dien:

- Man hinh dang nhap: nhan dien HR, vung mo ta, form tai khoan/mat khau.
- Dashboard tong quan: sidebar phan he, KPI, bieu do va trung tam thong bao.
- Quan ly nhan vien: bo loc, bang du lieu, form chi tiet va nut thao tac.

## Cach noi khi bao cao

"Phan UI/UX cua nhom em duoc phac thao tren Figma theo 3 man hinh chinh:
dang nhap, tong quan va quan ly nhan vien. Sau do nhom em hien thuc giao dien bang
WPF XAML trong `LoginWindow.xaml` va `MainWindow.xaml`."

Luu y: Neu chua import len Figma va chua co link Share, khong nen noi la da co minh chung Figma hoan chinh.
